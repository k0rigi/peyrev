Solutions · E-commerce

Automate your marketplace and store work.

Connections for Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Shopify and WooCommerce come ready; you only enter your own API details. For panels without an API, Peyrev uses the browser for you.

What can it do?

Work Peyrev takes on

  • Collect orders in one placeRead orders from marketplace services and write them to Excel, Google Sheets or your own database.
  • Stock threshold alertsChecks stock at set intervals and notifies purchasing via Telegram, Teams or email about products that fall below the threshold.
  • Bulk entry into panelsEnters the product or customer list from a sheet into a web panel row by row and writes each row's result back to the sheet.
  • Keep customers informedSend notifications via WhatsApp Business, SMS or email when an order or shipping status changes.
