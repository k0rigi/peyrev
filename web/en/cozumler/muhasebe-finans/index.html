Solutions · Accounting and finance

Hand repetitive accounting work to Peyrev.

Invoice entry from Excel into your ERP, archiving invoices from the inbox, a daily exchange rate table: tasks done with the same steps every day are set up once as a flow, then run on their own.

What can it do?

Work Peyrev takes on

  • Data entry into ERP screensWindow steps recognize the ERP's own screen, fill in the fields and press Save. They also work in desktop programs that have no API.
  • Invoice and document archiveReads the PDFs in incoming emails, extracts the amount or number, and moves each file to the right folder; anything it can't read is left for review.
  • Exchange rates and tablesReads a table from a web page or a service, writes it to Excel and notifies the team. Automatically every morning with the scheduler.
  • Reconciliation and reportsCompares lists with database query, Excel and CSV steps, reports the differences and sends them by email.

Ready-made templates

Download and try them now

Open a template in Peyrev with Akışlar → İçe aktar (Flows → Import), then adjust the file paths and connections to fit your work. All templates are on the Templates page.

  • Accounting

    Invoice entry: from Excel to your ERP

    Enters the invoices from Excel into the ERP's invoice screen row by row; notes any it can't enter, reports what was processed and emails accounting.

    11 steps. You need: an invoice list (Excel), the ERP's invoice screen, an email connection.

    Download template

  • Accounting

    Archive PDF invoices

    Reads the PDF of each invoice in the inbox; if it finds the amount, it moves the file to that month's folder, otherwise it leaves it for review.

    6 steps. You need: an email reading (IMAP) connection, an archive folder.

    Download template

  • Finance

    Daily exchange rates

    Reads the table on an exchange rate page, writes it to Excel and notifies the team. With the scheduler, it can run automatically every weekday morning.

    4 steps. You need: an Excel file to write the rates to, a notification channel set up in Settings (Ayarlar), e.g. Telegram.

    Download template
