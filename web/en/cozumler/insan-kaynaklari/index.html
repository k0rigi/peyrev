Solutions · Human resources and office

Save time on HR and office work.

Emailing payslips one by one, merging lists, entering the same details into several systems: Peyrev does this work on your computer, with your accounts.

What can it do?

Work Peyrev takes on

  • Payslip and document distributionLists the PDFs in a folder, finds each employee by the staff number in the file name and sends the document to their own email; notes any that don't match.
  • Merge listsReads Excel, CSV and Google Sheets files, merges them, runs calculations and brings everything into a single report.
  • Entry into multiple systemsEnters a new employee's details into web panels and desktop programs in one go.
  • Team notificationsSends messages to Microsoft Teams, Slack, Google Chat or Telegram, and sends reports by email.
