Features

Set up a manual task once. Peyrev repeats it.

The screens below were captured in Peyrev itself, using sample flows.

01 · Design

Pick steps from the library and the flow builds itself.

Add any of 108 steps to the diagram, across categories such as window, mouse and keyboard, screen, files and Excel, data, Google Sheets, databases, web, email and API. One step's output becomes a variable in the next; the flow reads left to right, from start to finish.

  • Step search and favorites
  • Create steps by recording your actions
  • Preview: try it without using the screen

02 · Connect

Conditions, loops and error paths, all in the diagram.

Open a flow as a diagram: the "Her satır için" (For each row) loop, the true and false paths of a "Koşul" (Condition), the "Hata olursa" (On error) block and "Adıma git" (Go to step) links are visible at a glance. Drag a box's connector to another box to change the path.

  • Trace of the last run, right on the diagram
  • Run up to the step you choose
  • Test a single step on its own

03 · Configure

Every step comes with instructions.

Click a step to open its settings and a short guide on the right. For click and type steps, you point at the target on screen; Peyrev recognizes the window and field from the app's own structure. If the target can't be found, the step stops. It never clicks on a guess.

  • Pick targets on screen
  • Copy variables with a click
  • Choose where to go after a step

04 · Schedule

Let flows run on their own, on schedule.

Run a flow at set times, every day, on the weekdays you choose or at intervals. The calendar view shows the week's plan, the next job and any overlaps; flows that use the screen run one after another, and those that don't run in the background.

  • Priority and latest start time
  • Catch up on runs missed while Peyrev was closed
  • Start another flow when one finishes

05 · Monitor

Every run leaves a trace.

Run history shows when each flow ran, how long it took and how it ended. The details include a step-by-step log and the files produced; show the run on the diagram to see exactly where it stopped.

  • Step-by-step run log
  • Download output files
  • Screenshot in the "Hata olursa" (On error) step

Agents add-on

Your digital team works in one office.

AI agents use the flows and abilities you give them, within the limits you allow. Watch your team in a 3D office: who took a task, who asked a question, who delivered the result.

  • 01

    The manager agent delegates

    You give the task to the manager agent; it splits the work across departments, answers questions and combines the results.

  • 02

    Only the flows you allow

    Each department agent runs only the flows you open to it; agents can't access each other's information without permission.

  • 03

    Irreversible actions need your approval

    Flows with steps that write, send or delete wait for your approval, unless you chose otherwise when granting access.

  • 04

    Your own model

    Agents work through your own AI model connection; tasks, cost and time appear on the performance screen.

Already have an account?

You sign in to Peyrev inside the app, not on this page: with your account's email address (or your username) and password.

Your license end date is shown in the app under Ayarlar → Peyrev lisansı (Settings → Peyrev license); you change your password there too.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us with the Öneri ve destek (Feedback and support) button in Peyrev.

Do I need to know how to code to use Peyrev?

No. Flows are built from steps you pick in the step library, and every step comes with instructions. For click and type steps, you point at the target on screen.

How much does it cost?

Peyrev is $10 a month, Peyrev + Agents is $20 a month, and Peyrev Digital Twin is $100 a month per site; prices exclude VAT. The subscription is monthly and you can cancel anytime; you keep using it until the end of the period you paid for.

Where do I download Peyrev?

Once your payment is confirmed, your personal download link arrives by email; the link is valid for 30 days. If it has expired or you can't find the email, write to info@peyrev.com and we'll send you a new one.

Do I need an internet connection to use Peyrev?

Yes. Your license is verified through peyrev.com each time the app opens, so opening it requires an internet connection. Your flows, connection settings and run records stay on your computer.

Can I use my account on more than one computer?

Your account is open on one computer at a time; signing in on another computer ends the previous session.

How do updates arrive?

The installed app checks for new versions on its own and verifies them as it downloads; your flows and settings are kept when it updates.

Do scheduled flows run while the computer is off?

No. The scheduler runs on this computer while Peyrev is open. If you choose, a run missed while Peyrev was closed is done once when it opens.

Which AI model do the agents use?

Agents work with the model you connect: OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic or a local model on your own computer. Your model connection key is kept in the operating system's secure vault.

How do I extend my license?

Fill in the application form with your account's email address and choose the plan you want. No new account is created and the password field does not change your current password; your request reaches us and we send you the payment details.

I was given a password code. What should I do?

On Peyrev's sign-in screen, choose Şifre kodum var (I have a password code) and enter your username, the code and a password of your choice. The code can be used once.

Which permissions are requested on a Mac?

Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions are requested so Peyrev can recognize and control windows. On Windows, Microsoft Edge WebView2 is required.
